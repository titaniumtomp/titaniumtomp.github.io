Platform draft

Ten planks under discussion

A working draft of ten planks, with each core idea kept separate from proposed wording. Sourced from the shared America First platform draft for revision inside the officers group.

Draft, not adopted. A work in progress for discussion and revision. These positions have not been formally adopted.

1. Closed borders

Core idea

Closed borders. Specific mechanisms are not yet decided.

Proposed wording

We decide who comes into this country. The law at the border should be enforced, and coming in illegally should stop. How we do that will be spelled out as the platform develops.

2. Balanced budgets

Core idea

Balanced budgets. Specific mechanisms are not yet decided.

Proposed wording

Families can't spend more than they take in forever, and neither should Washington. We will work to balance the federal budget and keep it balanced. The specific steps will be spelled out as the platform develops.

3. Jobs for Americans

Core idea

Jobs for Americans. Specific mechanisms are not yet decided.

Proposed wording

Work in this country should go to Americans, and a job should pay enough to support a family. We want good jobs built and kept here. The specific policies will be spelled out as the platform develops.

4. Energy

Core idea

Accelerate the transition to carbon-free power through nuclear and solar, recognize the economy runs on carbon-based fuels right now, and build more refining capacity during the transition.

Proposed wording

We want to speed up the move to carbon-free power, with nuclear and solar leading the way. Our economy depends on oil, gas, and other fossil fuels today, so the transition has to be honest about that. While we make the change, we need more refining capacity, not less, so fuel stays available and affordable.

5. Law and order

Core idea

We are a law and order party, and criminals should be in jail.

Proposed wording

We are a law and order party. Laws should be enforced. People convicted of crimes, after a fair trial and due process, should serve their time in jail.

6. Taxes

Core idea

A national sales tax with food and medicine exempt. A consumption-based system using a national sales tax, with the aim that people who spend more, including wealthy consumers, pay more tax on that spending. The rate and which current taxes it would replace are not yet decided.

Proposed wording

We want a tax system based on what people buy, through a national sales tax. Food and medicine would not be taxed. Our aim is that people who spend more pay more on that spending. The rate, and which of today's taxes it replaces, will be spelled out as the platform develops.

7. Abortion

Core idea

Abortion is a state's right issue.

Proposed wording

Abortion policy should be decided by each state, not by Washington.

8. Lobbying after Congress

Core idea

For members of Congress, a 10-year restriction from the time they leave office to the time they can lobby for other companies.

Proposed wording

Members of Congress should face a 10-year restriction from the time they leave office to the time they can lobby for other companies.

9. Congressional term limits

Core idea

Two terms per chamber: two terms in the Senate and two terms in the House of Representatives.

Proposed wording

We support a limit of two terms in the Senate and two terms in the House of Representatives. How these limits would take effect will be spelled out as the platform develops.

10. Innovation, technology, and manufacturing

Core idea

Plan for and embrace the future. Innovation and technology should pave the way for America to lead the world across fields, including manufacturing.

Proposed wording

We will plan for the future and embrace innovation and technology. We want America to develop new technologies, put them to work, and lead the world across industries and fields, including manufacturing. Building the future should mean making things in America and preparing Americans for the work ahead.

Source: America First platform draft · for discussion and revision only.
