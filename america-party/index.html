Platform draft

Thirteen planks under discussion

A working draft of thirteen planks, with each core idea kept separate from proposed wording. Sourced from the shared America First platform draft for revision inside the officers group.

Draft, not adopted. A work in progress for discussion and revision. All wording is proposed, not formally adopted. Open choices remain on implementation, costs, tradeoffs, and scope, including lawful entry rules, specific budget cuts, sentencing details, and how term limits would take effect.
1

Stop illegal entry, control legal entry

Core idea

Enforce the border and stop illegal entry, while controlling lawful entry.

Proposed wording

We decide who comes into this country. Enforce the law at the border and stop illegal entry. Legal entry should be controlled through a lawful process.

2

Balanced budgets

Core idea

Balance the federal budget through real spending cuts and hard choices. Identify what gets cut and show the savings.

Proposed wording

Balancing the federal budget will require real spending cuts and hard choices. We will identify what gets cut and show the savings, rather than promise balance without saying where it comes from.

3

Jobs for Americans

Core idea

Jobs for Americans.

Proposed wording

Work in this country should go to Americans, and a job should pay enough to support a family. We want good jobs built and kept here.

4

Energy

Core idea

Accelerate the transition to carbon-free power through nuclear and solar, recognize the economy runs on carbon-based fuels right now, and build more refining capacity during the transition.

Proposed wording

We want to speed up the move to carbon-free power, with nuclear and solar leading the way. Our economy depends on oil, gas, and other fossil fuels today, so the transition has to be honest about that. While we make the change, we need more refining capacity, not less, so fuel stays available and affordable.

5

Law and order

Core idea

We are a law and order party, and criminals should be in jail.

Proposed wording

We are a law and order party. Laws should be enforced. People convicted of violent crimes and repeat offenders should face jail after due process. For minor crimes, penalties should be proportionate and can include restitution rather than jail.

6

Taxes

Core idea

A national sales tax with food and medicine exempt. A consumption-based system using a national sales tax, with the aim that people who spend more, including wealthy consumers, pay more tax on that spending. The rate and which current taxes it would replace are not yet decided.

Proposed wording

We want a tax system based on what people buy, through a national sales tax. Food and medicine would not be taxed. Our aim is that people who spend more pay more on that spending. Open choices: the rate, which current taxes this replaces rather than adds to, and the transition rules. The design must address purchases made with previously taxed savings; no treatment has been chosen.

7

Abortion

Core idea

Abortion is a state's right issue.

Proposed wording

Abortion policy should be decided by each state, not by Washington.

8

Lobbying after Congress

Core idea

For members of Congress, a 10-year restriction from the time they leave office to the time they can lobby for other companies.

Proposed wording

Members of Congress should face a 10-year restriction from the time they leave office to the time they can lobby for other companies.

9

Congressional term limits

Core idea

Two terms per chamber: two terms in the Senate and two terms in the House of Representatives.

Proposed wording

We support a limit of two terms in the Senate and two terms in the House of Representatives.

10

Innovation, technology, and manufacturing

Core idea

Plan for and embrace the future. Innovation and technology should pave the way for America to lead the world across fields, including manufacturing.

Proposed wording

We will plan for the future and embrace innovation and technology. We want America to develop new technologies, put them to work, and lead the world across industries and fields, including manufacturing. Building the future should mean making things in America and preparing Americans for the work ahead. Expand access to trade education and apprenticeships alongside other education paths.

11

Healthcare and affordability

Core idea

Proposed addition: make healthcare costs easier to understand and compare, and improve affordability and access.

Proposed wording

People should be able to understand what planned care will cost before agreeing to it. Require clear prices before planned care and make it easier to compare providers. Open choices: how to address insurance costs, coverage, and access to care beyond price transparency.

12

Constitutional rights and equal treatment

Core idea

Proposed addition: protect speech, privacy, due process, equal treatment, and accountability for government abuse.

Proposed wording

Protect constitutional rights, including speech, privacy, and due process. Apply the law equally and hold government accountable when it abuses its power. Open choices: the safeguards and accountability procedures that will put these commitments into practice.

13

National defense and foreign policy

Core idea

Proposed addition: defend the country, define priorities abroad, and set a clear standard for military commitments.

Proposed wording

Make defending the country and protecting Americans the first priorities of national defense. Use diplomacy to address disputes and weigh military commitments against a clear purpose, costs, and an exit plan. Open choices: alliance obligations, standards for using force, and defense spending priorities.

Ben opened the session and kept branding, civic-idea, and later tax-and-spending threads moving. Tim (@RetiredYoungNW) brought logo concepts, a living platform draft, and an evening Google Doc compiling the group’s thoughts. Main threads

  • Branding & slogansTim shared logo and header concepts. Ben posted a red-white-and-blue eagle with a purple shield, meant to blend the two major parties; Tim later said he liked it, and Ben called it a temporary logo they can refine. Jason shared a “Free to Succeed. Fair for All.” poster and a later tweak. Slogan tries included “The party that brings people together,” “One country. Core values. Term limits.,” “One Country. Abundance for All.,” “Free to succeed. Fair for all.,” and “Personal Freedom, Shared Prosperity.” No slogan was chosen.
  • Principles & platformShared starting points: clean energy, domestic manufacturing, constitutional rights, fiscal responsibility, and unity. Devo asked for statesmanship, working across the aisle, and respect in disagreement; Tim agreed on respect but said core values should not be compromised. Jason asked that healthcare be in the draft (Tim said he added it) and called term limits non-negotiable. Others backed term limits, fair pay with controls, and no insider trading in Congress.
  • Immigration & civic ideasDevo suggested a competitive legal pathway to citizenship with yearly job-category quotas. Jason supported legal immigration and thought competition could help. Ben favored faster tracks for entrepreneurs and hard workers, with later check-ins. Serious crime was discussed as jail and/or deportation. A later idea was nationwide voter ID. None of these were adopted.
  • Tax & spendingIn the evening Ben sketched a broader tax-reform idea: equal federal tax treatment across states, a goods purchase tax, the same registration rules, ending property tax, no tax on already-taxed used items, no Social Security or pension tax, and no tax on assets after retirement. Jason joked about no tax on tips. The group talked about stopping excessive spending, applying a DOGE-style mandate at every level of government, and how hard it will be to balance the budget while debt exceeds GDP. Devo noted the last surplus was about twenty years ago and brief. Tim said if the goal is to save America, spending has to come under control even though it is hard. None of this was adopted.
